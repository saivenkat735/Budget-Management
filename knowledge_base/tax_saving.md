# Tax Saving Strategies in India

## Section 80C

Investments eligible for deduction include:

* Public Provident Fund (PPF)
* Employee Provident Fund (EPF)
* Equity Linked Savings Scheme (ELSS)
* Tax Saving Fixed Deposits
* Life Insurance Premiums

Maximum deduction: ₹1.5 lakh per financial year.

## National Pension System (NPS)

Additional deduction of ₹50,000 under Section 80CCD(1B).

## Health Insurance

Premiums paid for health insurance may qualify for deductions under Section 80D.

## Tax Planning Tips

* Start investments early in the financial year.
* Maintain documentation for deductions.
* Review tax-saving opportunities annually.
