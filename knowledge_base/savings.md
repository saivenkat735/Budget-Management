# Savings Strategies

## Pay Yourself First

Transfer money to savings immediately after receiving income before spending on discretionary items.

## Emergency Fund

Maintain an emergency fund covering 3 to 6 months of living expenses.

## Automate Savings

Set up automatic transfers to savings or investment accounts each month.

## Avoid Lifestyle Inflation

When income increases, increase savings and investments instead of increasing spending proportionally.

## Track Expenses

Review expenses monthly and identify unnecessary spending.
