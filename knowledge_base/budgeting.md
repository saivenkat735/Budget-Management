# 50/30/20 Budget Rule

## Definition

The 50/30/20 rule is a budgeting framework that divides after-tax income into three categories:

* 50% for needs
* 30% for wants
* 20% for savings and investments

## Benefits

* Easy to understand
* Encourages consistent saving
* Prevents overspending

## Example

If monthly income is ₹60,000:

* Needs: ₹30,000
* Wants: ₹18,000
* Savings: ₹12,000

## When to Use

Suitable for beginners who want a simple budgeting system.
